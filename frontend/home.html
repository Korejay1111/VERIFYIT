Our Mission

Why we built iConfam and what drives us forward

Combat Misinformation

Fake news spreads six times faster than accurate information. iConfam provides an accessible, intelligent first line of defense against the viral spread of misleading claims and fabricated stories across digital platforms.

Democratize Verification

Fact-checking should not be a privilege reserved for institutions. By combining multiple AI models into a simple interface, iConfam puts powerful verification capabilities into the hands of every internet user, regardless of technical expertise.

Transparency Through AI

Every verification result includes a detailed breakdown showing how each model contributed to the final score. We believe users deserve to understand the reasoning behind each assessment, not just the conclusion.

Speed Meets Accuracy

Misinformation waits for no one. Our multi-model pipeline delivers comprehensive analysis in under five seconds, ensuring that verification keeps pace with the rapid spread of online content.

Source Cross-Referencing

We go beyond text analysis by searching the web in real-time, cross-referencing claims against over 100 trusted news sources and fact-checking organizations to provide contextual verification.

Privacy First

Your data stays yours. iConfam processes content in real-time without storing inputs on our servers. Verification history lives locally in your browser, and you maintain full control over your data at all times.

Powered by Technology

The AI stack behind every verification

BERT NLP Model

Fine-tuned fake news detection model analyzing linguistic patterns, sentiment, and structural anomalies. Contributes 25% to the final score.

Groq LLaMA 3.1 70B

High-speed large language model providing contextual reasoning and claim assessment. The largest contributor at 35% weight in score fusion.

Web Search Engine

Real-time DuckDuckGo search and Google News RSS for cross-referencing claims against live sources. Weighted at 40% for maximum reliability.

Gemini 2.0 Flash

Google's multimodal vision AI for analyzing image content directly. Powers image verification with smart API key rotation for reliability.

EasyOCR

Optical character recognition engine that extracts text from uploaded images and screenshots, enabling verification of visual content.

Score Fusion Engine

Weighted combination algorithm that merges BERT (25%), LLM (35%), and Web (40%) scores into a single, calibrated credibility rating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about iConfam

iConfam uses a multi-model approach combining BERT-based natural language processing, large language model analysis via Groq, and real-time web search verification with a focus on global news sources. The BERT model evaluates linguistic patterns, the LLM provides contextual reasoning, and web search cross-references claims against trusted international and regional sources. These scores are weighted and combined for a final credibility rating.
Our multi-model approach achieves approximately 97% accuracy on known datasets. However, no automated system is perfect — we recommend using iConfam as a tool to guide your judgment, not as an absolute truth detector. Always cross-reference important claims with multiple trusted sources.
The credibility score ranges from 0 to 100. A score above 70 suggests the content is likely credible, 40-70 indicates uncertainty or mixed signals, and below 40 suggests the content may be unreliable or misleading. The score factors in BERT analysis (25%), LLM reasoning (35%), and web verification (40%).
